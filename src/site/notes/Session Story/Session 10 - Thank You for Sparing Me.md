The group left the [[Fanewick Cities & Towns/Celestial Gateway\|Celestial Gateway]] on day of the night cycle. As they traversed the forest, they did not notice any immediate change in the Witherwild, but they didn’t know how quickly the changes would happen now that the eye was gone.

After some time walking, the group found an abandoned camp. They searched the camp, but they found nothing of note. However, [[PCs/Ashkansi\|Ashkansi]] came across a tattered piece of gray robe. Upon further inspection, she noticed a crying eye. This was a symbol she recognized of the [[Reapers of Nikta/Reapers of Nikta\|Reapers of Nikta]], similar to the garb of the man she killed in the tavern in the [[Fanewick Cities & Towns/Stolen Fen\|Stolen Fen]]. Arthagorn found tracks that led away from the camp. With his scouting skills, he realized the tracks were likely about a day old and were heading toward the Cult of the Carrion Saint. Ash went invisible and followed the footsteps. As she did, an assassin broke through the forest into the clearing. 

This assassin saw Arthagorn and called out that the Ribbet was not the one they were looking for, but they’ll take it. A fight ensued. When Ash was no longer invisible, the assassins yelled to seize her, too. One after another, assassins were felled by the group. Only one was left at the end of the battle — a Witherwild infected fungril that somehow roused one of the dead during the battle. The action was clearly done against its own desires.

Koraxys approached the fungril and asked who he was. When she told him her name, he became emotional, apologizing for giving her what was likely a hard life. He informed her that he was [[Notable Figures/Fanewick NPCs/Aradon\|Aradon]], her father. Aradon told her that he was on his way back from a run for the black market when he was overtaken by a withered beat and became infected. When he returned to Drezza, the black market Infernis told him to go out with a group of assassins that were after what could be a potential cure since time was short, instructing him to use the cure as soon as they retrieved the package. This confirmed that the assassins were sent out because Ash had not met her time limit for return.

They give the box over to Aradon, but they realized he needed the vial that Ash took out. Ash gave him the vial but there was not immediate change. He thank them anyway and hoped it would help, but he admitted he didn’t know how much time he really had left. Koraxys convinced him to join them on their way back to [[Haven Cities & Towns/Beacon Pass\|Beacon Pass]].

Before they headed out, the sword that the group bought some time ago that was corrupted by the Witherwild began to grow vines that reached out toward Aradon. He realized it was his sword. He sat and removed the curse from the sword and his shield before he gave them to Ori as a thanks.

Heading out, the group navigated through the forest, finally breaking out into an open field on the other side of the [[Fanewick Cities & Towns/Riverwalker’s Hunting Ground\|Riverwalker’s Hunting Ground]]. In the distance they saw a campfire burning, surrounded by caravans, and decided to head over.
