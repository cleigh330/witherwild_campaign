[[PCs/Orielle\|Orielle]] and [[PCs/Caelum\|Caelum]] made their way to [[Aradon\|Aradon]]’s house to meet up with [[PCs/Koraxys\|Koraxys]] and [[PCs/Ashkansi\|Ashkansi]]. Inside the tossed house, Koraxys found two letters and read them aloud to the group.

---

“I find it abhorrent that I am even forced to write you this letter as the contents of which should need no written explanation, but your incessant and deliberate visits to our estate would signify otherwise. The guise of merchant trade routes has come to an end.

I know what you’ve done. I know you have tainted my family’s bloodline.

There is now much to repair internally, and this requires the immediate end to your trade route here. You are no longer welcome on the premises, and should any of our staff or family see you, guards will expeditiously handle the situation. You have been warned.

K.“

---

“Glad our mutual friend put us in touch.
Sorry to hear of your recent troubles. I could always find work for someone of your skill, however. Find me if you’re up for it. [[Haven Cities & Towns/Beacon Pass\|Beacon Pass]].”

The letter was signed with the symbol of an eye and Infernis tail that the group now knew belonged to the Black Market.

---

Upon reading these letters, Koraxys was clearly perturbed by this information since she still is unsure who her birth father truly is — only knowing it is a Fungril. The letter being signed “K” was an added layer of uncertainty. Could Aradon, in fact, be her father? He has been missing for roughly a week according to the shopkeepers in [[Fanewick Cities & Towns/Cordycep Cove\|Cordycep Cove]]. Could they find him at Beacon’s Pass now? With the advice of her party, Koraxys did not leave any note for Aradon and instead, left a flower that Caleum created for her.

Before leaving, Ash took a few minutes to open the locked box that she received from [[Zerrin\|Zerrin]]. She was meant to deliver this back to [[Drezza\|Drezza]] at the Black Market, but curiosity got the better of her. Inside the box were various red petals and vials with red, orange, and purple liquids. Ash gave the box to Caelum, and after spending a few minutes to inspect it, he found that one of the petals was from an actual Lady’s Veil flower. Caelum took that particular petal out, and he hopes to learn how to cultivate that flower on his own through his druid craft. Additionally, he noticed differences between the vials of liquid. The purple and orange vials were clearly distilled from what was likely just petals and herbs, but the red vial was different in its viscosity. It was thicker, suggesting it was an experimentation with a liquid similar to blood, if not actually blood. After taking the Lady’s Veil and returning the vials, Caleum gave the box back to Ash, and they put it into their chest of holding.

Traversing the wilderness on their way back to [[Fanewick Cities & Towns/God’s Nest\|God’s Nest]], they encountered some members of the cult and engaged in battle. When they defeated the cult members, Ori and Koraxys went up to the failed experiment. It was a giant that had body parts from other creatures sutured onto it. Ori immediately noticed the black ichor that stained the skin around the mouth and ears. Ash decided to take some of that ichor in a vial to study. Everyone realized that some of the body parts attached to this giant’s body looked similar to [[PCs/Willow\|Willow]] and [[PCs/Rizzha\|Rizzha]], leading them to believe the worst happened. 

Koraxys connected with the dead being, diving into its memory. She focused on the idea of rebirth. In the memory, it was like feeling through a void of nothingness. When the creature saw Raskil and the Infernis told it to follow, Koraxys felt the overwhelming need to obey. Then she was shunted out of the memory. Ori and Koraxys decided that no essence of the person was there after the transformation, so they decided to start to dismember the experiment.

As they were pulling apart sutures, [[_Assets/Cirelle\|Cirelle]] showed up. She told them she was too nervous to wait the extra day when she noticed they still weren’t at God’s Nest. She told them that she did find [[Faint Deities/Great Owl Nikta\|Great Owl Nikta]] at the [[Fanewick Cities & Towns/Celestial Gateway\|Celestial Gateway]]. There are statues of the deities at the Gateway, and Nikta’s statue was missing a crystal eye in the orbital socket, so it was confirmation the other crystal was their target. Her plan is to have Koraxys help her channel magical energy into the eye, Ori and Ash will keep watch on the perimeter, and Caelum will distract Nikta. Her goal is to not have to engage in any fight with the Faint Deity. 

![IMG_0824.webp](/img/user/_Assets/IMG_0824.webp)

While she was talking, the group noticed Cirelle appeared tired. Caelum saw that the arm she was holding the staff in had black tendrils slightly poking out through the armor wraps she wore.

In the middle of their discussion, rustling from the woods caught Koraxys and Cirelle’s attention. Sister Verona approached the group and greeted Cirelle, who was visibly displeased to see the cultist. Verona actually greeted Cirelle as the Fanewraith, suggesting they’ve encountered one another before. Sister Verona tells Cirelle she is disappointed to hear that she still does not understand that the Carrion Saint is coming and the Witherwild and rot will save them. Cirelle quickly turned to tell the group to head to the location they talked about and to not do actions until she was there. Turning back to Sister Verona, Cirelle turned into a panther and lunged at her. They fell back into the forest out of sight.
