Kriel Dirn is the Spymaster in [[Haven Cities & Towns/Haven\|Haven]], serving [[Notable Figures/Haven NPCs/Emperor Niclis Ilineiros\|Emperor Niclis Ilineiros]] as his eyes and ears in the shadows. He runs a den of spies that do whatever necessary to serve and protect the Haven empire. Above all, Dirn gives his loyalty to the crown and its people; however, many who know of him and his den of spies do not care for him or his work. They find him manipulative and untrustworthy. Despite their beliefs, he views himself as a force for the greater good.

As an elf, his physicality is rather lean, and while is physical form may not be threatening, his stern and serious demeanor could be imposing. Kriel Dirn has a light blueish gray skin tone and white hair pulled back away from his face, accentuating sharp cheekbones and crystalline blue eyes.

Serving as Spymaster for as long as he has, Dirn is skilled at finding those to work for him, and he has been particularly adept at using those low on their luck, which is how he found a certain Druid, young and unversed in city life but held great promise. [[_Assets/Cirelle\|Cirelle]] worked under Kriel Dirn for many years. He utilized her ability to disguise herself as animals to eavesdrop, and, luckily for him, she proved herself useful with persuasion and intimidation. Over the years, the two grew close, and Cirelle became Dirn’s right hand.

When the Serpent’s Sickness was on the rise and the cure of the Lady’s Veil was discovered, [[Notable Figures/Haven NPCs/Archmage Phylax\|Archmage Phylax]] informed Dirn of his plan to take the Reaping Eye from the [[Faint Deities/Great Owl Nikta\|Great Owl Nikta]]. Kriel Dirn was not a fan of the Archmage, viewing him as pompous, and did not always trust him, but the Emperor put a great deal of trust in Phylax. Dirn shared Phylax’s plan with Cirelle, perhaps divulging more information than wise, and Cirelle immediately and vehemently objected. She begged him to let her speak to the Emperor and the Archmage in order to dissuade them and find another solution. She warned of the unforeseen consequences of throwing off balance. But, Dirn knew that there was no use. Niclis and Phylax would not listen to a common spy, an outsider, when their people where suffering and the Emperor’s reputation and safety lay on the line. Determined to save those in Fanewick and put a stop to a plan already in motion, Cirelle called Dirn a coward and left the guild.

The loss of his best spy left an unsettling feeling in Kriel Dirn’s gut, and even after acquiring Nikta’s Eye, he was left uneasy. His feelings proved validated when he heard talk of someone going by the title “Fanewraith,” and by descriptions of deeds, he knew it must be Cirelle. Thus, he found a band of adventures that all had unique ties to Haven that he believed could be swayed to work for him and stop the Fanewraith by whatever means necessary, promising each of them something useful. For [[PCs/Orielle\|Orielle]], she would be welcomed back to Haven and be allowed to serve in the Haven Army again should she choose. For [[PCs/Ashkansi\|Ashkansi]], she would be given clemency and no longer pursued by the Thieve’s Guild or his spies. For [[PCs/Koraxys\|Koraxys]], she would be able to live in the city without fear of her family and learn alongside Haven mages.

Did Kriel back Phylax’s plan for the greater good of Haven with no doubts? Does he want Cirelle brought back to him alive to reconcile their parting and make her see reason or to apologize for his inaction? Does he care if she is killed in the process? One cannot know for certain.
