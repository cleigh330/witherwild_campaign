The group headed toward the [[Fanewick Cities & Towns/Celestial Gateway\|Celestial Gateway]] through the forest. [[Notable Figures/Fanewick NPCs/Cirelle\|Cirelle]] scouted ahead and confirmed Nikta was there but was facing her statue, which they needed to approach. So, Cirelle sent out Arthagorn with Briar, [[PCs/Orielle\|Orielle]], and [[PCs/Ashkansi\|Ashkansi]] to scout. Then, [[PCs/Caelum\|Caelum]] went out to begin talking to the [[Faint Deities/Great Owl Nikta\|Great Owl Nikta]] to distract her and get her away from the statue.

When Caelum walked toward Nikta, he greeted her the same way as the vision. Old friend. She asked if it was really *him*, but Caelum’s words were not enough to persuade her. Nikta charged toward him and transported the two of them into an alternate dimension. It was an open field with just the two of them, and above them, instead of sky, was roots of plants hanging down. While there, she began to ask him why and how he got there. As they talked, Nikta asked him if he would be upset if his life was made forfeit in order to restore balance back to the world. Caelum stated that would not bother him if it brought back peace to the natural world.

While the two were talking, their physical bodies were encapsulated by swirling golden light. Ori moved in from the perimeter to watch more closely over her friend, but did not notice any rustling the forest some odd yards away. 

At the same time, Farasha, Cirelle, and [[PCs/Koraxys\|Koraxys]] were by the statue. They were pouring magic into the remaining crystal eye, but with each push, Cirelle’s agony grew and the black crept all the way up her arms toward her shoulders. With another push, Cirelle collapsed to her knees and cried out in pain.

The shout from Cirelle tore Nikta out of her realm with Caelum. She swiftly turned and shot out a beam toward the group at her statue. It knocked Koraxys back, Farasha flew out of the way, and Cirelle was hit and seemingly unconscious or dead. Seconds after that happened, Ash, invisible, sent out shadows to hold Nikta down. 

Unrelenting and angered, Nikta continued to attack the group, and when Ori tried to move around Caelum to put Nikta in a chokehold, Caelum stopped her, sprouting tree branches, roots, and vines between Ori and the Faint Deity. Eventually, after a few more shots back and forth, Farasha was able to calm Nikta for a moment.

When tensions cooled and they explained some of their plan, Nikta aided Cirelle. Nikta mentioned that she heard Cirelle’s prayers to her and admitted that she ignored her. Cirelle shared why they were here, and Nikta somewhat begrudgingly gave her the remaining her. As she did, her other eye turned into a black vacuous hole, and the once powerful god crumpled to the ground.

[[Faint Deities/Hyacynis\|Hyacynis]], as a fawn, appeared at the edge of the statue lined Gateway. Furious at the scene, she called Nikta and the group all fools before rushing back into the forest as a large deer. No one followed after.

Nikta told Cirelle that “this” would kill her. Cirelle’s response was simply “I know.” Telepathically, Nikta transmitted information to her, and she then telepathically gave Caelum a vision as well. 

The vision was of an unknown building, likely a basement, of stone and glowing blue lights. Two voices were present, one very clearly [[Notable Figures/Haven NPCs/Kriel Dirn\|Kriel Dirn]]. Soon, Caelum realized he was the Reaping Eye of Nikta in the hands of the [[Notable Figures/Haven NPCs/Archmage Phylax\|Archmage Phylax]]. The Archmage told Dirn that they were the only two with keys to this room, and after unlocking it with a key, he dispelled a magical ward on the door. He placed the eye on a pedestal, and across from it, Caelum was looking at the other half of the glowing stone soul of [[Faint Deities/Shun’Aush the Granite Ophid\|Shun’Aush the Granite Ophid]]. The other half of himself. Dirn stated that they should be concerned about having just lost someone so close to their order, but Phylax insisted that Dirn worried too much about his little pet. A guard called out to them, stating that someone needed assistance with a spell, and the two sealed the door and left, ending the vision.

Nikta informed Caelum that he now knew as much as she did, and he shared this vision with the group. She also offered to the group that they could stay and rest up if they’d like before heading out.

Cirelle, with her strength renewed strength, let the group know that she was going to take the eye to [[Brennok\|Brennok]] in [[Fanewick Cities & Towns/Alula\|Alula]] for safe keeping. She would meet them at the Crooked Bell in [[Haven Cities & Towns/Haven\|Haven]]. She left, and the group readied themselves for departure. Nikta sat down by her statue and began to create a cocoon of branches around herself before it turned to light and she vanished from sight.
