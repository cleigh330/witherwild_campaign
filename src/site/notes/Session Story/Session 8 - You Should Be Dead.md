[[_Assets/Cirelle\|Cirelle]] in her panther form toppled on top of Sister Verona and out of sight through the overgrown Witherwild forest. The group regarded one another uneasily before deciding to follow the Fanewraith into trees. They followed blood splatters through the brush until they found a wounded panther on the ground. [[PCs/Orielle\|Orielle]] kept a perimeter while [[PCs/Caelum\|Caelum]] healed the panther, and once the wounds closed, Cirelle turned back to her human form and thanked Caelum.

During this time, [[PCs/Ashkansi\|Ashkansi]] and [[PCs/Koraxys\|Koraxys]] continued to trail the blood to find Sister Verona. They realized the blood was bringing them back toward the cult, and, while still keeping some distance, they could see a crowd surrounding what they presumed to be Sister Verona. After some debating, they tried to send in Koraxys’s ghostly servant to finish off Sister Verona, but the plan went awry. The cult was onto some oddities happening in the forest and began to search, so Ash and Koraxys left and headed back to Cirelle.

Upon their return, they found that two of Cirelle’s closest companions from [[Fanewick Cities & Towns/Alula\|Alula]] had found her from a telepathic message she sent out. [[PCs/Farasha & Arthagorn\|Farasha & Arthagorn]], a fierce Infernis Seraph and a skilled Ribbet Ranger, were never far from Cirelle, and they showed in what she believed could have been her end if not for Caelum. Though this wasn’t the end of the line for her, Caelum did notice that the black tendrils were growing up the arm she used to grip the staff. Before much more could be said besides some quick introductions, Ash and Koraxys let the group know that they should all head out of there because they might have the Cult of the Carrion Saint on their tail. 

Together, with Cirelle and their two new companions, the group headed to the [[Fanewick Cities & Towns/Celestial Gateway\|Celestial Gateway]]. Cirelle was visibly slower than the rest. They were on their way to cross the river they previously traversed when they had to defeat a group of withered beasts. Afterward, they rested. Caelum made a salve for Cirelle’s arm, but she told him that she didn’t think it would do much. When he asked her to try, she removed the wraps from her arm, allowing everyone to see the black tendrils shooting up her arm. The others in the group offered to hold the staff for her, but she denied them, telling them it was her burden. She also mentioned that it only afflicted her when she used the staff anyway, so for now, she would be okay. Before everyone took their rest, Cirelle pulled Farasha and Arthagorn to the side and told them the plan for [[Faint Deities/Great Owl Nikta\|Great Owl Nikta]]. Additionally, she told them to keep Caelum alive because he was their hope — the hope that all of this could work out.

Thankfully, they were able pass through the river easily since they had the rope set up from their first trek, and Farasha was able to fly across with Cirelle. 

Heading into the forest, they saw a large deer watching them. Its large antlers were covered in flowers, and bees buzzed around its mantle. The deer slowly approached them, and the group held still. As it came closer, Ash realized this deer was actually [[Faint Deities/Hyacynis\|Hyacynis]]. This Faint Deity sniffed Caelum’s chest plate, shook its head, and started to walk away. In his head, Caelum heard the question “how?” Caelum tried to telepathically reply, but there was no response. When he asked what the Faint Deity meant aloud, it answered in his head again that it did not believe it was really “him” because “he should be dead.” Then Hyacynis left.

Caelum told the group what was said, and Cirelle said that technically, the deer wasn’t wrong. Tired of Cirelle dancing around the questions, Ash cast a spell that would force Cirelle to tell the truth. Under the spell, Cirelle told them everything she’d been theorizing as to why Caelum was so special. She believed him to be half of the soul of [[Faint Deities/Shun’Aush the Granite Ophid\|Shun’Aush the Granite Ophid]]. If Nikta saw her friend was somehow still “living,” then there was hope that all of this could resolve peacefully. There was no knowing what would happen if something happened to Caelum. She explained that this was why Caelum would be the one to distract Nikta while they worked. Satisfied, Ash dropped the spell and they headed toward the Celestial Gate.

As they moved on, Cirelle walked next to Farasha and asked her if she still had her back through all this. Farasha told her she did as long as she told her and Arathagorn the whole truth without hiding important details.
